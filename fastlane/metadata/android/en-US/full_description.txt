An open-source client for real-time Bucharest public transport, built on the
open data from mo-bi.ro, the official TPBI GTFS feed and OpenStreetMap. It is a
de-Googled alternative to the official InfoTB app, which relies on Google Maps
and Play Services.

Features:

* Live vehicles on the map as line-number badges, refreshed every 30 seconds.
* A lines tab searchable by line number or by terminus; selecting a line draws
  every variant of its route, including branches, with travel-direction arrows.
* Stop markers from zoom 13 up. Tapping a stop lists every line that serves it,
  in one chronological timeline: live arrival times where the feed has them,
  the next scheduled departures otherwise.
* A full-day timetable grid per line, computed on device from the official GTFS
  archive.
* Optional traffic and road-incident layers, off by default.
* Nearby stops, sorted by distance from your last known location.
* Romanian and English interface.

The line, geometry and stop database is built on device from the official TPBI
GTFS archive, cached locally and refreshed when it is older than a day. A
bundled snapshot makes the first launch usable straight away.

No account, no tracking, no Google Play Services. It needs internet access for
live data, and optionally location access for the nearby-stops list.
